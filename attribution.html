Editorial apparatus

Sources & method

This is a working illustrated reading edition. The French text is based on the public-domain Curiosités esthétiques transcription distributed through French Wikisource, with section structure checked against OBVIL. The English text is a fresh, editable machine translation made paragraph by paragraph from that French source.

Annotations

Baudelaire’s own notes are marked Baudelaire. Modern notes are separately marked Editorial note · draft; they are research suggestions, not part of Baudelaire’s text, and are intentionally easy to remove after editorial review.

Images

Artwork and artist images are research aids assembled from museum, library, archive, auction, and public-domain sources. Where an exact work has not been located, the site uses the Baudelaire “NOT FOUND” placeholder rather than silently substituting another object.
